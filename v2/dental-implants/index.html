Overview

Replacing one tooth, several teeth or a full arch.

Replacing missing teeth is about more than filling a gap. We consider your bone, bite, appearance, function and long-term maintenance.

Different situations call for different solutions: a single dental implant, an implant-supported bridge or All-on-X full-arch implants.

What to expect

We discuss your concerns, examine your oral health and use 3D imaging and digital scans to assess your jawbone, teeth and bite before planning treatment.

Final restoration and ongoing care

Once healing is complete, your final restoration is fitted, followed by regular reviews and maintenance. Questions later? Call us on +60 3 8940 9393.
